Here's everything you need to get in touch with Uncle J's Dirty Apron BBQ or find us in person.

## Contact

The fastest way to reach us is through our website:

👉 **[unclebbq.com/contact](https://links.unclebbq.com/contact)**

Fill out the contact form and a member of our team will get back to you within **1–2 business days**.

For urgent matters — especially anything related to an order in progress — please call us directly. The phone number is listed on our contact page.

---

## What We Can Help With

- Order issues (wrong item, missing item, delivery problem)
- Refund requests
- Catering inquiries
- Pitmaster Rewards questions
- Press and media requests
- General questions and feedback

---

## Location

We're located in **Joliet, IL**. Exact address, parking info, and a map are available on our [contact page](https://links.unclebbq.com/contact).

---

## Hours

Current hours are posted on the [contact page](https://links.unclebbq.com/contact) and kept up to date. Hours can vary on holidays and for private events — if you're making a special trip, it's worth checking before you head over.

---

## Online Ordering Hours

Online ordering follows kitchen hours. When the kitchen closes, online ordering closes. If you see a message that ordering is temporarily unavailable, check back soon or plan ahead with a scheduled order next time.
