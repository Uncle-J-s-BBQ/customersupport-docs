Uncle J's Dirty Apron BBQ takes your privacy seriously. This article explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

---

## What Data We Collect

### Account Information
If you create an account, we store:
- Your name and email address
- Your phone number (if you add one)
- Your account creation date

Passwords are **never stored by us** — they are managed by Clerk, our authentication provider.

### Order History
We keep records of orders placed through our website. For financial and legal compliance, order records are retained even after account deletion — however, your personal details (name and email) are removed from them.

### Pitmaster Rewards
If you're enrolled in Pitmaster Rewards, we store:
- Your points balance and tier
- Your transaction history (points earned and redeemed)

### Marketing Subscriptions
If you subscribe to our email list, we store:
- Your email address
- Your subscription status and date

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email or by visiting [unclebbq.com/subscribe](https://links.unclebbq.com/subscribe).

### Payment Information
We **do not store payment details**. All payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or retain your card number.

---

## Your Rights

You have the following rights over your personal data:

### Right to Access
You can request a full summary of the personal data we hold on your account at any time.

### Right to Deletion
You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data.

---

## How to Exercise Your Rights

Visit the **Privacy Rights Portal** at [unclebbq.com/privacy-rights](https://privacyrights.unclebbq.com).

After signing in, you'll be offered two options:

- **📧 Email me my data** — We'll email you a complete summary of your personal data. Requires email verification (and phone, if linked to your account).
- **🗑️ Delete my account** — Permanently removes your account and all personal data. Requires email, phone, and password verification.

Both actions are irreversible. Account deletion in particular cannot be undone.
