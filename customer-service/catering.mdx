Uncle J's Dirty Apron BBQ is available for catering events — corporate lunches, family reunions, birthday parties, backyard cookouts, and more. We bring the pit to you.

---

## What Counts as a Catering Order?

Any order with a **food subtotal over $250** is automatically classified as a catering request. When you reach that threshold at checkout, you'll see a "Request a Catering Quote" prompt instead of a standard checkout button.

This threshold exists because large orders require advance coordination — we want to make sure we can smoke enough food to serve you properly.

---

## How to Request a Catering Quote

1. **Build your order** on [unclebbq.com](https://links.unclebbq.com/home) as normal — add items to your cart until it reflects what you need
2. When your subtotal exceeds $250, click **"Request a Catering Quote"**
3. Fill out the request form — include your event date, headcount, and any notes
4. We'll reach out within **1–2 business days** to confirm details, pricing, and logistics

## What We Offer

We can accommodate most events with advance notice. Typical catering setups include:

- **Bulk protein orders** — brisket, pulled pork, ribs, and chicken by the pound
- **Full spreads** — proteins + sides + bread for a complete meal
- **Drop-off catering** — we deliver, you serve
- **Setup catering** — we set up a full serving station at your venue (availability varies)

---

## What We Need from You

- **Event date and time** — the earlier you contact us, the better
- **Headcount** — so we know how much to smoke
- **Dietary needs** — let us know about allergies or dietary restrictions ahead of time
- **Delivery or pickup** — we can deliver to most locations in the Joliet area; larger events may require pickup

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How far in advance should I book?**
For events under 50 people, at least **3–5 days** notice is appreciated. For events over 50 people, **1–2 weeks** is ideal. Holiday weekends book up quickly — contact us as early as possible.

**Is there a minimum order size for catering?**
Yes — catering requests start at $250 in food. For very large events, we'll provide a custom quote.

**Do you handle setup and breakdown?**
Drop-off catering is our standard offering. Setup and staffed service is available for select events — let us know what you need when you reach out.

**Can I earn Pitmaster Rewards points on a catering order?**
Points are not automatically applied to catering quotes. Contact us and we can add points to your account manually once the order is confirmed.
