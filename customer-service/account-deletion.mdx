You have the right to permanently delete your Uncle J's Dirty Apron BBQ account and all personal data associated with it. This process is irreversible — please read carefully before proceeding.

---

## What Gets Deleted

When you complete an account deletion request, the following data is **permanently removed**:

- ✅ Your login account (email address and password)
- ✅ Pitmaster Rewards account and all accumulated points
- ✅ Saved delivery addresses
- ✅ Email marketing subscriptions
- ✅ SMS marketing subscriptions
- ✅ Pitmaster Helper AI chat history

## What Is Retained

The following data is **retained for legal and financial compliance** but is fully anonymised — your name and email are removed from it:

- Order records (required for tax and accounting purposes)

---

## How to Request Deletion

Account deletion requires multi-step identity verification to protect you from unauthorised deletion requests.

### Step 1 — Go to the Privacy Rights Portal

Visit **[privacyrights.unclebbq.com](https://privacyrights.unclebbq.com)**

### Step 2 — Sign In

You must be signed in to your Uncle J's account. If you are not signed in, a login prompt will appear on the page.

### Step 3 — Verify Your Identity

You will be walked through up to three verification steps:

1. **Email verification** — A 6-digit code is sent to your account email
2. **Phone verification** *(if you have a phone number on your account)* — A 6-digit SMS code is sent to your number
3. **Password confirmation** — Enter your account password

### Step 4 — Review & Confirm

You will be shown a summary of everything that will be deleted. To confirm, you must type **DELETE MY ACCOUNT** in the confirmation field.

### Step 5 — Done

Your account is permanently deleted. A farewell confirmation email is sent before deletion completes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I recover my account after deletion?**
No. Deletion is permanent and immediate. Your points, addresses, and login cannot be restored.

**What if I forgot my password?**
Use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page at [unclebbq.com](https://links.unclebbq.com/home) to reset it before starting the deletion process.

**Will I still receive emails after deletion?**
No. Your email address is removed from all marketing and transactional lists immediately upon deletion.

**How long does deletion take?**
The process completes instantly once confirmed. The confirmation email typically arrives within a few minutes.
