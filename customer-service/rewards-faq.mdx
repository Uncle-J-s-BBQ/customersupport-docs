## Earning Points

**How many points do I earn per order?**
You earn **5 points for every $10 spent** on eligible food and beverage items. Points are rounded down to the nearest whole number.

**What's included in the eligible subtotal?**
Points are based on your food and beverage subtotal only. Delivery fees, taxes, tips, and discount code deductions are excluded.

**When do my points show up?**
Points are credited to your account automatically after your order is confirmed. This typically happens within a few minutes. If your points haven't appeared after 24 hours, [contact us](https://links.unclebbq.com/contact) with your order number.

**Do I earn points on catering orders?**
Points are not automatically applied to catering orders over $250 (those go through a quoting process). Contact us once your order is confirmed and we can add points manually.

**Can I earn points as a guest without an account?**
No — you must be signed in to a Pitmaster Rewards account to earn points. If you placed a guest order and want to claim points, [contact us](https://links.unclebbq.com/contact) within 48 hours with your order reference number and we'll see what we can do.

## Expiry & Resets

**Do points expire?**
Points are valid for **12 months** from the date they were earned. Any qualifying purchase resets the expiration clock on your entire balance.

**What happens to my points if I don't order for a while?**
If 12 months pass without a qualifying purchase, expired points are removed from your balance. Your tier level may also reset accordingly.

**If I earn new points, does that reset the timer on old ones?**
Yes — any qualifying purchase resets the 12-month timer on your entire balance, not just the new points.

---

## Redeeming Rewards

**How do I redeem my points?**
Redemption offers are emailed to you as you hit milestones. Watch for discount codes and free-item offers tied to your tier level. You cannot manually "spend" points at checkout like a currency.

**Can I combine a reward code with a promotional discount code?**
No — only one discount code can be applied per order.

---

## Account Issues

**My points are wrong / I think I'm missing points.**
[Contact us](https://links.unclebbq.com/contact) with your order number and we'll review your transaction history and correct any errors.

**I deleted my account. Can I get my points back?**
No — account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All points and account history are deleted and cannot be recovered.

**Can I transfer my points to another account?**
Points are non-transferable.
